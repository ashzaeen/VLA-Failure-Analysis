# Literature

Papers this work builds on. The papers are not copied into this repository; follow the links or search the titles.

## The Model and Benchmarks

- **π0: A Vision-Language-Action Flow Model for General Robot Control.** Physical Intelligence (Black, Brown, Driess, et al.), 2024. [arXiv:2410.24164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24164). The model studied here: a PaliGemma vision-language backbone with a 300M-parameter action expert using conditional flow matching.
- **LIBERO: Benchmarking Knowledge Transfer in Lifelong Robot Learning.** Liu, Zhu, Gao, Feng, Liu, Zhu, et al. NeurIPS 36, 16186–16209, 2023. The simulated benchmark; the LIBERO-Object suite is used here.
- **LIBERO-Plus: A Progressive Robustness Benchmark for Vision-Language-Action Models.** CVPR 2026, pp. 38574–38583. Used in the spring robustness study.
- **LIBERO-PRO: Towards Robust and Fair Evaluation of Vision-Language-Action Models Beyond Memorization.** Argues that standard benchmarks overestimate ability because policies memorize training configurations.
- **LIBERO-X: Robustness Litmus for Vision-Language-Action Models.** A five-level progressive evaluation protocol.
- **robosuite: A Modular Simulation Framework and Benchmark for Robot Learning.** Zhu, Wong, Mandlekar, Martín-Martín. [arXiv:2009.12293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.12293).
