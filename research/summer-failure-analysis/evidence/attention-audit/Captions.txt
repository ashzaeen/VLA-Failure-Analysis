Representative attention-heatmap episodes — from the FULL 100-episode baseline run
(transformers 5.3.0, LIBERO-Object, beta=30). Each video overlays the model's last-layer
action attention on the base (overhead) and wrist cameras per timestep; the green circle marks
the single most-attended patch. The counts below come from a visual audit of ALL 10 trials per
object (per-object contact sheets in contact-sheets/).

01-orange-juice-grounded-success.mp4   [task 9, episode 4]
   Textbook grounding: the base-camera attention peak sits directly on the orange juice bottle
   (peak ~0.31). Audit: peak ON the target in 10/10 trials. Grasp succeeds.

02-alphabet-soup-diffuse-attention-success.mp4   [task 0, episode 0]
   Succeeds despite diffuse base-camera attention (peak ~0.07) whose hottest patch sits on the
   background, not the soup can. Audit: peak OFF-target (background) in 10/10 trials, with 10/10
   success — a consistent shortcut signature (success without looking at the target).

03-chocolate-pudding-wrist-carried-success.mp4   [task 8, episode 0]
   Base-camera attention is diffuse in all 10 trials; by the grasp the pudding box is framed near
   the center of the wrist camera in ~8/10 trials. NOTE (honesty): wrist-camera attention
   magnitude is ~0, so this is read as the wrist VIEW (via the approach trajectory) plausibly
   carrying the grasp — an INTERPRETATION, not evidence that the model attends to the target there.

04-milk-distractor-split-failure.mp4   [task 7, episode 6 — a genuine failure]
   Milk fails in 2/10 trials. In the failures the base-camera attention is more diffuse and split,
   with a competing hotspot away from the target and the arm never reaching the carton; successful
   trials concentrate more tightly on one object. The effect is real but modest, and clearest in
   this episode.

Summary: pi0 grounds distinctive objects (orange juice) reliably (10/10 on target), does not always
need to look at the target to succeed on generic objects (soup: 10/10 off-target yet 10/10 success),
and shows more diffuse/competing attention on the trials that fail (milk).
